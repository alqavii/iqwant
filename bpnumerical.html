Numerical Reasoning

Data-interpretation questions on energy-industry tables and charts: refinery runs, export volumes, freight and segment results. Each run generates fresh numbers. Reports put BP's numerical test at roughly 18–25 questions in about 25 minutes, under a minute and a half each, with a calculator allowed.

Use a calculator, as you would in the real test. Five options per question. The skill being tested is picking the right figures and the right operation fast, not the arithmetic itself.
