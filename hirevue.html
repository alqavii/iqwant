BP · Recorded video interview

HireVue-style Interview Simulator

BP confirms that recorded video interviews are part of its early-careers process. This runs the same shape: a question appears, you get a short preparation window, then recording starts automatically and stops when time runs out. Recordings stay in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Prep sites report about 30 seconds to prepare and up to 3 minutes to answer per question, with 5–7 questions. Some candidates report being allowed a re-record; others don't get one, so practise one-take answers. Topics reported for BP: why BP, why trading/this business area, the energy transition, teamwork, adapting to change, overcoming obstacles.

Settings

Camera check

No camera? You can still run it as a timed spoken practice; you just won't get playback.

Review

Watch yourself back

Score each answer honestly against the checklist. Assessors reportedly look at motivation, communication and how well your examples show the qualities asked about.
