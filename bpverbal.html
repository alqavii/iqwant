Verbal Reasoning

Short passages on energy markets and operations. For each statement, decide whether it is True, False, or Cannot say based only on the passage. 30 statements, 20 minutes.

The rule that catches people: use only what the passage says. Outside knowledge doesn't count, even if you're sure it's correct. "Cannot say" means the passage neither confirms nor contradicts it. Watch for absolute words (always, all, only) and for statements that swap cause and effect.
