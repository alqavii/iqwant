Interview Simulation

Market Making & Adverse Selection Game

The core interactive game asked in Jane Street, SIG, and Optiver interviews. You are the market maker providing two-way quotes on an unknown underlying distribution.

Interview Game Rules & Principles:

  • Contract Definition: You will quote on a contract with an uncertain final settlement value (e.g. sum of rolls, hidden deck parameters).
  • 2-Way Market: In each round, you must quote a [Bid] @ [Ask] with a maximum spread constraint (Max spread = 2.00).
  • Counterparties: Simulated market participants (both retail noise traders and informed smart-money traders) will decide whether to buy from you, sell to you, or pass.
  • Inventory & Adverse Selection: If informed traders constantly hit your bids or lift your offers, you have adverse selection! Skew your market to manage inventory.
  • Settlement: After 8 trading rounds, the true underlying value is revealed and your inventory is settled.
