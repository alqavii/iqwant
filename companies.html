Trading Firm Assessment Directory

Comprehensive breakdown of the exact online tests, gamified cognitive screens, technical interviews, and passing benchmarks across every tier-1 proprietary trading firm, market maker, and global physical commodity house.

Optiver

Amsterdam / Chicago / London / Sydney Options Market Maker & Prop Primary OA: 80 in 8
Stage 1: Online Assessment
80 in 8 Mental Math
80 mental arithmetic questions in 8 minutes. Negative marking (+1 correct, -1 wrong, 0 skip). Strict passing benchmark ~55-60+ points. Accuracy is prioritized.
Stage 2: Probability Test
Beat the Odds Assessment
20-30 probability & combinatorics questions (CodeSignal). 90 seconds per question with +1 / -1 scoring. Focus on expected value, dice, and symmetry.
Stage 3: Inductive & Memory
SHL Verify Interactive
Switch Challenge (rule deduction on odd/even and arrows) and Grid Memory Challenge. Evaluates working memory capacity and cognitive switching latency.
Stage 4: Superday
Trading Pit & Market Making
Interactive market making simulations with senior traders, mental math under pressure, options Greeks reasoning, and direct behavioral stress interviews.

Key Success Factors

  • Zero guessing in 80 in 8: A wrong guess costs 2 points relative to getting it right (+1 vs -1). Skip anything taking longer than 7 seconds.
  • Mental stamina: The assessment is designed to induce cognitive fatigue. Train with our built-in Optiver 80 in 8 simulator.

Desk Culture & Values

Direct, feedback-driven, highly meritocratic. Optiver prizes candidates who acknowledge mistakes instantly rather than defending a bad position. High competitive drive without ego.

Jane Street

New York / London / Hong Kong / Amsterdam Quantitative Trading & Tech Primary Focus: Probability & Games
Stage 1: Technical Phone Screen
Probability & EV Puzzles
45-minute live interview solving 2-3 probability puzzles. Conditional expectations, Bayes' rule, random walks, and Fermi estimation problems.
Stage 2: Strategy Interview
Market Making with Cards
Interviewer draws cards or defines a game. You quote bid/ask spreads. Evaluates your Bayesian updates, adverse selection awareness, and risk sizing.
Stage 3: Superday
3-4 Technical Rounds
Collaborative problem solving. OCaml / algorithmic puzzles for software roles; game theory, martingales, and complex probability for traders.

Key Success Factors

  • Think aloud constantly: Jane Street cares more about how you adapt to interviewer hints and handle uncertainty than instant correct answers.
  • Intuition over formal proofs: Seek symmetry arguments, edge-case checks, and sanity checks on calculations.

Desk Culture & Values

Deeply collaborative, academic, intellectual humility. No individual trader commissions; firm-wide bonus pool ensures traders freely share edge. OCaml-first technology stack.

Citadel & Citadel Securities

Miami / Chicago / New York / London Hedge Fund & Market Maker Primary OA: Pymetrics & HackerRank
Stage 1: Pymetrics Mini-Games
Cognitive & Risk Battery
12 gamified tests including BART Balloon risk test, Digits memory span, Keypress effort, and Arrow reaction to build a behavioral profile.
Stage 2: Quantitative Screen
HackerRank & Math
High-rigor math, linear algebra, multivariable calculus, and algorithmic coding in C++ or Python. Focus on time complexity and numerical stability.
Stage 3: Superday
Multi-Round Technical Depth
Intensive 4-6 interviews with portfolio managers and quant researchers. High-frequency market microstructure, statistical arbitrage, and deep data intuition.

Key Success Factors

  • Extreme precision: Citadel expects flawless execution on fundamental math and algorithms with zero tolerance for sloppy hand-waving.
  • BART Balloon Strategy: Demonstrate healthy, disciplined risk appetite without loss-chasing (train on our BART simulator).

Desk Culture & Values

High performance, fierce competition, extreme accountability. Unmatched technology infrastructure and capital scale. Results-driven reward model.

Trafigura

Geneva / Singapore / Houston / London Global Physical Commodity House Oil, Petroleum Products, Non-Ferrous Metals
Stage 1: Aptitude Screening
Numerical & Commercial Judgment
Fast mental calculations involving currency conversions, metric ton to barrel conversions, interest carrying costs, and scenario judgment.
Stage 2: Commercial Case Study
Cargo Logistics & Demurrage
Scenario test: Cargo shipment from West Africa to Rotterdam. Calculate freight chartering economics, laytime, demurrage risks, and basis spread hedging.
Stage 3: Assessment Centre
Physical Trading Simulation
Group commercial negotiation, blending optimization, storage tank arbitrage (contango plays), and rigorous interviews with physical desk heads.

Must-Know Commodities Mechanics

  • Full Carry & Storage Arbitrage: $F_0 = S_0 + \\text{storage} + \\text{interest} + \\text{insurance}$. Know when to buy spot, store, and short forward.
  • Incoterms: Differences between FOB (buyer handles vessel), CIF (seller delivers to destination), and DES.
  • Hedging: Hedging physical cargo price risk with ICE Brent futures while managing basis risk.

Desk Culture & Values

Commercial savvy, resilience, high interpersonal relationship focus. Physical commodity trading requires negotiating with shipowners, terminal operators, and refiners.

Vitol & Glencore

Rotterdam / Geneva / London / Singapore Energy & Mining Giants Crude, Products, Gas, Coal, Copper
Stage 1: Online Screening
Numerical Aptitude
High speed arithmetic, spread calculations, percentage margin calculations, and logical reasoning tests.
Stage 2: Trading Desk Interview
Refining & Crack Spreads
Deep technical questions on 3:2:1 refinery crack spreads, sweet vs sour crude discounts, pipeline transit times, and clean vs dirty tanker freight.
Stage 3: Superday
Commercial Pit Scenarios
Live trade execution dilemmas: How do you react when a canal is blocked, a refinery trips offline, or a counterparty defaults on a cargo nomination?

Core Interview Questions

  • "A refiner has $700k of oil financing at 7% annual interest. What is the carrying cost for 45 days?"
  • "Explain why deep backwardation cannot be arbitraged via reverse cash-and-carry once inventories hit tank bottoms."
  • "How does the IMO 2020 low-sulfur mandate impact the spread between Brent (sweet) and Urals (sour)?"

Desk Culture & Values

Sharp, commercial, fast-acting. Strong emphasis on downside risk containment and P&L ownership. Traders think in barrels, metric tonnes, and freight routes.

Flow Traders & IMC Trading

Amsterdam / Chicago / New York ETF Arbitrage & Options Market Makers Matrix Reasoning & Speed Math
Stage 1: Speed Arithmetic
8-Minute Math Test
Fast mental calculations involving fractions, percentages, powers, and squares. High passing accuracy required without scratchpad.
Stage 2: Abstract Reasoning
Matrigma / Raven's Matrices
Progressive geometric matrix problem solving under strict time limits to measure pure inductive reasoning and pattern recognition.
Stage 3: Superday
ETF Arbitrage & Option Spreads
Creation/redemption mechanism of ETFs, calculating iNAV (indicative Net Asset Value), tracking error, and fast market making pricing.

Key Success Factors

  • ETF Microstructure: Understand how ETF market makers arbitrage deviations between ETF share price and underlying basket NAV.
  • Mental speed: High comfort with percentages (e.g. 17.5% of 240) and fraction conversions.

Desk Culture & Values

Dynamic, high-energy Amsterdam trading floor vibe. Collaborative trading desks with rapid execution and instant debriefing after market close.

SIG (Susquehanna International Group)

Bala Cynwyd (Philly) / New York / Dublin / Sydney Options Market Making & Game Theory Poker & Decision Making Under Uncertainty
Stage 1: Online Assessment
Quantitative Reasoning
High-paced arithmetic, probability word problems, and estimation questions designed to test logical deduction under time pressure.
Stage 2: Phone Interview
Expected Value & Betting Logic
Interactive probability games with the interviewer. Pot odds, expected value, pot-limit betting scenarios, and sizing bets based on edge.
Stage 3: Superday
Poker & Game Theory Day
Play poker with traders, market making on unknown card distributions, group decision-making exercises, and deep probability brainteasers.

Key Success Factors

  • Master Poker Concepts: Pot odds, implied odds, fold equity, and bluff-catching frequencies. SIG literally trains new hires with full-time poker coaches!
  • Adverse Selection Awareness: If someone wants to bet against your line, ask: "What do they know that I don't?"

Desk Culture & Values

Legendary for its structured trading education program. Scientific approach to probabilistic games. Focus on rational process rather than short-term luck.
