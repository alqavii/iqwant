Beat the Odds: Kelly Criterion Simulator

Simulate sequential risk-taking across 20 stochastic bets. Quant interviewers test whether you maximize geometric bankroll growth while strictly avoiding ruin.

The Mathematical Law: The optimal fraction $f^*$ to wager on a bet with win probability $p$ and payout odds $b$ (to 1) is given by the Kelly Criterion: f^* = \frac{b \cdot p - q}{b} where $q = 1 - p$. Betting more than $f^*$ (over-betting) decreases your long-term growth and exponentially accelerates drawdown risk. Betting on negative EV ($b \cdot p < q$) is an immediate fail.
