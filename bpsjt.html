Situational Judgement Test

Twenty workplace scenarios set in a supply, trading and shipping business. For each, mark the most effective and the least effective response. This is the most/least format candidates report from BP's screen.

What's being judged. BP's stated values are safety, respect, excellence, courage and one team. In trading, add the non-negotiables: compliance, honest communication of risk and P&L, and escalating fast. The best answer is usually the one that fixes the problem and involves the right people, not the most cautious or the most heroic one.

Scoring. 1 point for each of most and least that matches the key (40 max). The scenarios and key are original practice material, not BP's own questions.
