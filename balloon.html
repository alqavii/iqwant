Balloon Analogue Risk Task (BART)

The standard psychological risk assessment utilized by Citadel (Pymetrics), Optiver, and IMC to measure your risk tolerance, loss aversion, consistency, and Expected Value (EV) discipline under uncertainty.

Citadel Securities Optiver IMC Trading Macquarie Pymetrics 12 Mini-Games

How It Works & Quant Trader Evaluation:

  • You will play through 30 balloons across 3 probabilistic color classes (Yellow, Blue, Red).
  • Each pump inflates the balloon and adds $0.05 to your temporary round earnings.
  • Each balloon has a hidden pop threshold. If it pops, you lose the accumulated money for that balloon.
  • Click "Bank / Collect" at any moment to secure your earnings into your permanent bank and advance to the next balloon.
  • Quant Secret: Trading algorithms measure your Adjusted BART Score (average pumps on unpopped balloons) and your tilt/loss aversion (whether you drastically under-pump immediately after a burst). Consistent, mathematically sound risk-taking beats both hyper-cautious play and reckless gamble.
