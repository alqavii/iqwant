90% Confidence Interval Calibration

Elite quant interviewers evaluate whether your stated confidence matches reality. Overconfidence is a fatal flaw in market makers.

The Calibration Principle: For each question, provide a range [Lower Bound, Upper Bound] such that you are 90% confident the true value falls inside. In a test of 10 questions, exactly 9 of the 10 true values should fall within your intervals. If fewer than 9 fall inside, you are overconfident (your bands are too narrow for your uncertainty). If all 10 are inside with absurdly wide bands, you are underconfident.
